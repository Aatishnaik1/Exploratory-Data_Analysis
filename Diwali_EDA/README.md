# 🪔 Diwali Sales EDA Project

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Diwali sales dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior, product performance, sales trends, and key revenue-generating segments.  
The goal is to uncover business insights that can help optimize marketing strategies, target the right audience, and improve overall sales performance during the festive season.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This analysis focuses on identifying:
- Who buys the most during Diwali (gender, age group, occupation)
- Which states generate the highest revenue
- Popular product categories and top-selling items
- Customer spending patterns
- Data cleaning, preprocessing, and visualization

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Information

The dataset contains customer demographic details and shopping behavior.  
Typical columns include:
- Customer ID  
- Gender  
- Age Group  
- State  
- Occupation  
- Marital Status  
- Product Category  
- Product ID  
- Orders  
- Amount (Purchase Value)

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Female customers** purchased more than male customers.  
- **Age group 26–35 years** has the highest purchase frequency.  
- **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka** generated the highest sales.  
- **Married women** in the 26–35 age group were the major buyers.  
- **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation** professionals spent the most.  
- **Food, Clothing, and Electronics** were the top-performing product categories.  

These insights help businesses target the right segments more effectively during festive seasons.
